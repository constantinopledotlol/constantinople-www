Rules

There are only two:

1. No duping items.
Duping will not get you banned, but your ender chest, inventory and location are wiped. You start over with nothing, nowhere.
2. No doxxing.
Sharing anyone's real-world information earns a mute. You can still play; the chat just no longer hears you.

Everything else is permitted. Grief, raid, trap, betray, or build in peace. There is no staff intervention beyond the two rules above.
